2. How to apply to these companies

All of these companies have an active agreement (MOA) with your school for BSCS/BSIT OJT, so the school route is the fastest.

  1. Talk to your OJT coordinator first. Tell them which companies you're applying to and ask for an endorsement letter for each.
  2. Prepare your files: a one-page résumé (PDF), the endorsement letter, and the application letter from this page. Name files like DelaCruz_Resume.pdf.
  3. Send it to the contact person shown on each company's card. Use the email version of the letter. If there's no email, call the number or bring printed copies to the office on a weekday morning.
  4. Track it here: tick "I applied". If they call you, add the interview date and time.
  5. Follow up after 5–7 working days with a short, polite message if you haven't heard back.
  6. Apply to 5–8 companies at once. Small companies take fewer interns, so don't wait on one reply.
